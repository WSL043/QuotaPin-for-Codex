# QuotaPin brand policy

The software license covers code and documentation. It does not grant permission to make a modified build look like an official QuotaPin release.

Unmodified binaries published by the official project may keep the QuotaPin name and logo. A modified distribution may truthfully say that it is based on QuotaPin and link to the official source, but it should use a different product name and icon and clearly identify itself as unofficial.

This policy is meant to help users distinguish the free official project from repackaged or misleading copies. It does not reduce the rights granted by the software license, restrict honest compatibility statements, or prevent forks from existing.
